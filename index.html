  1. 2026Independent research, ongoing

    leakproof

    A backtest can run without a fault and still report a number that depends on something it must not. leakproof works out each value's footprint, the data cells it was computed from, from the program text alone. Four readings of that footprint catch look-ahead bias, a forecast scored on its own outcome, a result picked as the best of many tries, and prices adjusted in hindsight.

    Every result is graded tradable, out-of-sample or in-sample best of K, lists what it took on trust, and gets a certificate anyone can re-check without the data. The guarantees are machine-checked in Rocq with no axioms.

    Python, pandas, RocqCode

    benchmark leaks rejected, with all 89 corrections accepted

    computed from the benchmark table in the paper draft

  5. Aug 2025Personal project

    Multi-agent market simulation

    A tick-based stock exchange in C++ where autonomous agents trade through per-stock limit order books with market orders, limit orders and partial fills. Matching crosses the best bid and ask from price-priority heaps and sets each tick's price to the volume-weighted fill price. Strategies are written in C++ or in Python through an embedded interpreter, up to an LSTM price predictor.

    C++23, CMake, OpenMP, pybind11, PythonCode

  6. Jun 2025Personal project

    Particle life simulator

    A real-time artificial-life simulation in C where 800 particles of 8 types self-organize into cell-like clusters under random attraction and repulsion rules, rendered with Allegro 5. Each frame bins particles into a 14×14 grid with a counting sort, then computes forces per cell over its 3×3 neighbourhood in parallel with OpenMP. The result is the same on 1 thread or 8.

    C, OpenMP, Allegro 5Code

    19.7 ms

    mean wall-clock time per frame, forces and rendering included

    computed from the simulator's own frame timer over a 12-second run
