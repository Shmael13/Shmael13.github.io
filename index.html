Quantitative finance

  • Independent research
    2026

    leakproof

    A Python library that tags every value in a research pipeline with the time its information becomes available, and rejects any position that depends on the future, with the offending dependency path, before a backtest runs. It covers look-ahead bias, full-sample preprocessing, survivorship and leaky cross-validation as one rule. It comes with a paper draft and an AST linter.

    Zero soundness violations across 10,000 randomly generated pipelines, and all 12 of 12 leakage patterns caught in a benchmark on synthetic, equity and crypto data.

    Python, NumPy, pytest, LaTeXCodePaper draft
  • Personal project
    2025

    Multi-agent market simulation

    A tick-based stock exchange in C++ where autonomous agents trade through per-stock limit order books with market orders, limit orders and partial fills. Strategies can be written in C++ or in Python through an embedded interpreter, from trend-following to an LSTM price predictor.

    Matching crosses the best bid and ask from price-priority heaps and sets each tick's price to the volume-weighted fill price, running in parallel across stocks with OpenMP.

    C++23, CMake, OpenMP, pybind11, PythonCode

Machine learning

  • Rice COMP 559
    with Victoria Liu
    2026

    Sentiment dynamics on LLM agent graphs

    An async simulator that runs LLM agents over a directed graph, scores every message for agreement with an NLI cross-encoder, and folds the score into signed edge weights. We used it to test whether structural balance theory holds for LLM triads.

    In an 80-round, four-agent debate on a fully connected graph, all 241 messages flowed through just 6 of 12 possible edges, every one touching the same agent.

    Python, asyncio, Hugging Face Transformers, NetworkX, OllamaCodeReport
  • Rice COMP 584
    2026

    Emergent communication with hierarchical transformers

    A sender and a receiver, built from the byte encoder, word backbone and byte decoder of hierarchical autoregressive transformers, invent their own byte-level language to pick out objects. They are trained end to end with straight-through Gumbel-softmax, and the resulting language is measured for compositionality and Zipf-like statistics.

    97% accuracy on held-out objects when choosing among 8 candidates, where chance is 12.5%.

    Python, PyTorchCode
  • ELEC 537
    2025

    Activity recognition from accelerometer data

    Classifies one-second windows of 50 Hz, 3-axis accelerometer data as moving or sleeping, comparing classical models on hand-built window features against 1D CNN, LSTM and GRU models on the raw sequences.

    An RBF SVM on 14 window features reached 93% on held-out recordings, well ahead of the deep models trained on only nine recordings.

    Python, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/KerasCode

Systems and simulation

  • Personal project
    2025

    Particle life simulator

    A real-time artificial-life simulation in C where 800 particles of 8 types self-organize into cell-like clusters under randomly generated attraction and repulsion rules, rendered live with Allegro 5.

    Each frame, particles are binned into a 14×14 spatial grid with a counting sort, and forces are computed per cell over its 3×3 neighbourhood in parallel with OpenMP.

    C, OpenMP, Allegro 5Code
