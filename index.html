leakproof

A backtest replays a trading idea on old data to see how it would have done. It can run without a single error and still cheat, by quietly using information that didn't exist yet at the moment of a trade.

leakproof reads a strategy's code, without running it or looking at the data. It works out exactly which data every number was computed from, and rejects any result that peeks ahead. Results it accepts get a grade and a certificate others can re-check without the data. The core guarantees are proved in the Rocq proof assistant.

Python, pandas, RocqCode

SIM EXCHANGEAUG 2025 

      

        

          
Multi-agent market simulation

          
A tick-based stock exchange in C++ where autonomous agents trade through per-stock limit order books with market orders, limit orders and partial fills. Matching crosses the best bid and ask from price-priority heaps and sets each tick's price to the volume-weighted fill price. Strategies run in C++ or in Python through an embedded interpreter, up to an LSTM price predictor.

          
101 TRADERS

          
following 7 different rules moved the price from $10.00 to $16.13 in 300 rounds. Nobody sets the price; it comes out of their orders

          
computed from one logged 300-round run of the simulator

          
C++23, CMake, OpenMP, pybind11, PythonCode

Personal project, June 2025

        
Particle life simulator

        
A real-time artificial-life simulation in C where 800 particles of 8 types self-organize into cell-like clusters under random attraction and repulsion rules, rendered with Allegro 5. Each frame bins particles into a 14×14 grid with a counting sort, then computes forces per cell over its 3×3 neighbourhood in parallel with OpenMP. The result is the same on 1 thread or 8.

        
C, OpenMP, Allegro 5Code
